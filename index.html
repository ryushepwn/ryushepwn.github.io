01 about

I do offensive security work: web and API testing, recon automation, and exploit development. Most of it happens in bug bounty programs, where the interesting part is rarely the payload — it's mapping the surface and understanding what the application quietly trusts.

The rest of the time I'm writing tooling so the next hunt starts further ahead: recon pipelines, request replay, and small agents that handle the repetitive parts. Everything here is built and maintained with heavy automation; the notes and PoCs are checked before they go out.
